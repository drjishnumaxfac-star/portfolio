## Update: two honest listings (replaces the "SketchRoot as cover" idea)

You asked for listings that mostly show the clinical services, with SketchRoot added so they aren't rejected. I'd avoid that. A directory that reviews "startups" would be getting a clinic dressed up as one, which is the thing its review exists to catch. It risks rejection, a ban, or removal later, and it blurs both brands. Use two clean listings instead:

1. **SketchRoot listing (startup directories).** SketchRoot is the product and the 50% early-access offer is the hook. The founder line can honestly say you are a practising oral and maxillofacial surgeon in Kochi, with a link to doctorj.in. That puts your clinical credibility in front of reviewers without misdescribing the product.
2. **doctorj.in listing (professional, portfolio and doctor directories).** The clinical services lead. Mention SketchRoot in one line under "also by Dr. Mohan".
