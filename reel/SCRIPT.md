# SketchRoot early-access launch reel: script

**Format:** 1080×1920 vertical reel, 30 fps, 68.8 s
**Music:** the soundtrack from the SaaS brand video (rights held by Dr. Jishnu Mohan), extended to 68.8 s by repeating its 39 s phrase on a downbeat (`public/music-launch.m4a`)
**Goal:** get dental aspirants to **follow @doctorj.in and DM "EARLY ACCESS"**. Early access goes to the first 5,000 followers, with dental students verified and given priority.
**Source of truth for claims:** sketchroot.com (founder note, FAQ, exams list)

Every scene cut lands on a musical phrase downbeat (3.12 s + 6 s × n). The beat map was
detected with `npx hyperframes beats beatmap-launch` (drop at about 7.2 s).
