# SketchRoot early-access launch reel: script

**Format:** 1080×1920 vertical reel, 30 fps, 56.7 s (the full length of the music track)
**Music:** the soundtrack from the SaaS brand video (rights held by Dr. Jishnu Mohan)
**Goal:** pull NEET MDS, INI CET and INBDE aspirants to `sketchroot.com` early access, and get comments
**Source of truth for claims:** sketchroot.com (founder note, FAQ, exams list)

Every scene cut lands on a musical phrase downbeat (3.12 s + 6 s × n). The beat map was
