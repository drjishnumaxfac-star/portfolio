# SketchRoot early-access launch reel: script

**Format:** 1080×1920 vertical reel, 30 fps, 74.8 s
**Music:** the soundtrack from the SaaS brand video (rights held by Dr. Jishnu Mohan), extended to 74.8 s by repeating from its 33 s phrase on a downbeat (`public/music-launch.m4a`)
**Goal:** a three-step funnel. **Step 1:** follow @doctorj.in on Instagram. **Step 2:** DM "EARLY ACCESS". An automated reply checks the follow and sends the link. **Step 3:** join early access on sketchroot.com with a Gmail address. Early access goes to the first 5,000 followers, with dental students verified and given priority.
**Source of truth for claims:** sketchroot.com (founder note, FAQ, exams list)

Every scene cut lands on a musical phrase downbeat (3.12 s + 6 s × n). The beat map was
detected with `npx hyperframes beats beatmap-launch` (drop at about 7.2 s).
