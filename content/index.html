Every device in this portfolio began as a problem encountered during a surgery, observed, measured, and re engineered between cases.

What are the subspecialities of maxillofacial surgery? Maxillofacial surgery treats the mouth, jaws, face and neck. Its main subspecialities are head and neck oncology and reconstruction (oral cancer and jaw tumours), orthognathic or corrective jaw surgery, temporomandibular joint (TMJ) disorders, facial trauma, dental implantology including complex and zygomatic implants, cleft and craniofacial surgery, oral pathology, and surgery for snoring and obstructive sleep apnea. Most patients need only one of these; knowing which one applies to you is the first step of a consultation.
